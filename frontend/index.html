How ParkSmart Works

From pre-booking your slot to contactless exit in 4 intuitive steps.

1

Locate & Select

Pick your preferred facility (e.g. City Center Mall) and select an available bay on the interactive floor canvas.

2

Confirm & Pay

Select your registered vehicle, time window, and confirm booking with automated rate calculation.

3

Drive In with RFID

Seamless Departure
